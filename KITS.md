# Hands-On Training Kits

A purchasable kit (or free online toolset) to get hands-on with every skill in the tree.

> **Prices are approximate (USD) and change** - always confirm with the vendor. Items marked
> *(online/free)* are software, datasets, or courses rather than a physical product. Do genetic-
> modification work only in a legal, registered space with appropriate biosafety oversight; some
> skills (mammalian culture, flow cytometry) realistically need a shared BSL-2 / core facility.
